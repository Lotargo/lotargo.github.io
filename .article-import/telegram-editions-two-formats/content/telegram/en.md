# One topic, two editions

Telegram previously received only a short announcement: a title, description, and button. It worked technically, but the channel remained a notification feed while the linked discussion group repeated almost the same content.

## What changed

- the website keeps the complete Russian and English editions;
- Telegram receives a separately edited version;
- Markdown becomes **bold text**, *italics*, lists, quotes, and `inline code`;
- the cover is published with the text;
- one channel post keeps one discussion thread.

> The Telegram edition can now stand on its own without replacing the blog.

The blog still contains the complete article, responsive layout, galleries, and bilingual navigation.
